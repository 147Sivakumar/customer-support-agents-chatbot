# Account and Login Help

## Forgotten Password
1. Open the sign-in page.
2. Select "Forgot Password".
3. Enter the registered email address.
4. Follow the password-reset link sent by email.
5. Create a new password and sign in again.

## Login Problems
- Check that the registered email address is correct.
- Check that the password is entered correctly.
- If the reset email does not arrive, check the spam folder.
- If the customer still cannot access the account, escalate to human support.

## Account Security
- Never ask customers to share their passwords.
- Suspicious account activity must be escalated to human support.
