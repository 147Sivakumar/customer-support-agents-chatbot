# Refund and Return Policy

## Return Window
- Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused and in their original packaging to qualify for a standard return.

## Refund Process
1. The customer contacts support with their order ID.
2. Support checks return eligibility.
3. Eligible returns are reviewed and processed by the support team.
4. Refund timing depends on the payment provider.

## Damaged Products
- Customers should provide their order ID and describe the damage.
- Replacement or refund requests must be reviewed by support.

## Important
- Do not promise a refund before eligibility is confirmed.
- Escalate disputed or complicated payment cases to human support.
