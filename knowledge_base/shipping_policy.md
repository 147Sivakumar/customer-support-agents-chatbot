# Shipping Policy

## Standard Delivery
- Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days after dispatch.
- Customers receive a tracking number by email after dispatch.

## Delayed Orders
- Customers can check shipment status using their tracking number.
- If an order has not arrived by the expected delivery date, support should check tracking information.
- If tracking information is unavailable or the delay cannot be resolved, escalate to human support.

## Shipping Issues
- Customers should provide their order ID when contacting support.
- Missing or damaged deliveries should be reviewed by the support team.
