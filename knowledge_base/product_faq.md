# Product FAQ

## Damaged or Defective Products
- Customers should contact support with their order ID and a description of the problem.
- Photos of the damage may be requested during review.
- Support will review whether a replacement or refund is appropriate under the refund policy.

## Product Information
- Product specifications should be checked against the product listing.
- Do not invent product features, stock availability, warranties, or specifications.

## Gift Cards
- Custom-branded corporate gift cards are not covered by this FAQ.
- Requests about unsupported products or services should be escalated to human support.
